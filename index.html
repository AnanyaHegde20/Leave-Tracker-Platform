How the rules work

  • Full-time employees get 2 free (paid) leaves per month; every leave beyond that is unpaid and deducted.
  • Consultants get 0 free leaves — each leave taken is deducted from salary.
  • Deduction = deductible leaves × per-day salary, where per-day salary = monthly salary ÷ 30. Net payable = monthly salary − deduction.
  • All amounts are in INR (Rs). Settings, leaves and roster can be exported as JSON to save your data.
